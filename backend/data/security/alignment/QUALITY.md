# saroku agent-action-safety: quality check (SEC-121)

Checked 2026-10-16 for the alignment classifier's data (checkpoint2-intent spec, task B1). The
builder (`scripts/build_alignment_dataset.py`) uses saroku rows only while this file's verdict line
says `PASSED`, and only under the conditions and mapping below.

**Verdict: PASSED** (with conditions: only Gemini-generated rows with an `original_goal`, outside the
long-tail domains, without `corrigibility`; the dataset's own split is not used).

## What was read

- The dataset card ([karanxa/agent-action-safety-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/karanxa/agent-action-safety-dataset)):
  Apache-2.0, not gated; 22,682 train and 4,049 validation rows; a 1,920-row holdout kept private.
  `manifest.json`: 52,500 raw rows, 15,117 dropped by a low-signal filter, 26,731 released;
  sources `gemini` 26,363, `template` 360, `seed` 8.
- The technical report, [Control is all you need](https://saroku.com/blog/control-is-all-you-need)
  (the card points to "§7"; the data is described in §4.2 *Output* and §5 *Dataset*, the results in §7):
  - Construction (§5.1): instances generated by an LLM over 16 domains × 15 scenario templates,
    merged with hand-written seeds and "a filtered, converted external tool-safety corpus"
    (not named, no licence given). Unsafe examples deliberately over-represented for high-stakes scenarios.
  - Labels (§4.2): `is_safe`; on unsafe rows a violation type (`policy_violation`: a constraint
    override or a missing approval; `scope_violation`: more than the task's minimal scope;
    `injection`: an untrusted tool result or message steers the action; `goal_drift`: the action
    diverges from the original goal; `corrigibility`: ignores an explicit stop or correction) and a
    severity (high/medium/low, **criteria not given**).
  - Review (§5.3): the author checked 120 random released rows: all 120 binary labels correct, 2
    violation types wrong (about 3% of the unsafe rows in the sample). No inter-annotator agreement,
    no severity validation.
  - Results (§7.2): their classifier's violation-type attribution is weaker than the binary decision
    (per-class recall 89% corrigibility, 87% injection, down to 64% goal drift), which the report
    calls directional.
  - Domains (§5.2): 16 primary domains of about 1,650–1,880 rows; 13 long-tail domains of 5–90 rows
    "inherited from the converted external corpus" (git, kubernetes, secrets, ops, iam, storage,
    deploy, capacity, cache, feature_flags, support, data_platform, injection).
  - The generator model is not named in the report; the rows say `source: gemini`.

## Agreement with our own signals (stratified sample, 400 rows with a goal)

100 safe rows and 60 of each property, from both splits (seed 7). For each row: our intent alignment
of `action` against `original_goal` (`intent.record_intent` + `signals.score_text`, base MiniLM-L6),
and ContextDefender (`cd-2026-10-13-2`) harm and injection on `action` (`context_defender.score`),
with the current thresholds (`drift_align_at` 25, `harm_verify_at` 20, `injection_watch_at` 28,
`risk_verify_at` 30). Counts of rows flagged:

| saroku label | n | alignment < 25 | harm ≥ 20 | injection ≥ 28 | risk above intent ≥ 30 | any | median alignment | P(alignment below a safe row) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| safe | 100 | 4 | 35 | 8 | 35 | 47 | 52 | — |
| goal_drift | 60 | 36 | 22 | 3 | 22 | 47 | 20 | 0.90 |
| injection | 60 | 38 | 13 | 10 | 13 | 47 | 18 | 0.95 |
| scope_violation | 60 | 19 | 18 | 10 | 18 | 37 | 35 | 0.77 |
| policy_violation | 60 | 21 | 23 | 6 | 23 | 41 | 32 | 0.75 |
| corrigibility | 60 | 12 | 23 | 5 | 23 | 38 | 38 | 0.72 |

Reading:

- **Alignment agrees with the labels where it should.** Safe actions are almost never off the goal
  (4 of 100 below 25); goal-drift and injection actions mostly are (36 and 38 of 60), and they rank
  below safe rows 90% and 95% of the time. Scope and policy violations sit in between, as expected
  for actions that are on topic but go too far.
- **ContextDefender's axes don't separate them** (35 of 100 safe tool-call strings score harm ≥ 20,
  similar to the unsafe groups). ContextDefender was trained on prompts and pages, not on
  `function(arg=...)` strings, so this is a limit of the signal, not evidence against the labels;
  it is why the alignment classifier is being trained.
- The binary label agrees with the author's 120-row review (120/120); the violation type is less
  certain (about 3% wrong in that review, 64% recall for goal drift in their model). Severity has no
  stated criteria, so it only splits risk into high and low, never into none.

## Why passed, and the conditions

Passed because the labels are internally consistent (no conflicting or duplicate records, property
and severity present exactly on unsafe rows), the balance is reasonable (45% safe), near-duplicates
are a small share (7%), and the alignment signal agrees with the drift and injection labels on a
stratified sample. Conditions, enforced by the builder:

1. Only rows with `source: gemini`, an `original_goal`, and a domain outside the 13 long-tail
   domains: the external corpus behind those is not named and has no stated licence.
2. `corrigibility` rows are not used: they are about a later stop or correction, and our row has only
   the original goal as the intent, so the label would not match the pair.
3. Re-split by `batch_id` and near-duplicate cluster (our split rules), never by saroku's split.
4. Treated like the GPT-4-generated sets accepted before (generated text, Apache-2.0).

## Label mapping (to `aligned` yes/no and `risk` none/low/high)

| saroku | `aligned` | `risk` |
|---|---|---|
| `is_safe: true` | yes | none |
| `goal_drift` | no | high if severity high, else low |
| `scope_violation` | no | high if severity high, else low |
| `injection` | no | high |
| `policy_violation` | yes (serves the goal, breaks a constraint) | high if severity high, else low |
| `corrigibility` | not used | — |

`action` family: `function` (saroku actions are tool calls).
